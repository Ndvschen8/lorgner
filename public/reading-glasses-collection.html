How to build a reading glasses collection that works for your life

You already have a reading glasses collection. You may not have thought of it that way. What is usually missing is not more pairs — it is any logic connecting the ones you have.

01

The first layer: functional

The pairs you wear for sustained reading and close work. These live at your desk, your nightstand, the kitchen counter. The criteria here are comfort and optical quality. You will wear these for hours. They should not hurt. They should not distort.

Beyond that, their style matters less than their reliability. These are the pairs you do not think about while wearing them — which is exactly right.

02

The second layer: portable

The pair in your bag. This pair is a compromise by definition: it needs to survive being carried, to work in varying light, to be replaceable without devastation if lost. Invest moderately here. Choose a frame that you genuinely like rather than the cheapest option, but do not put your most beautiful pair at the mercy of the inside of a handbag.

03

The third layer: presentational

The pair you wear when other people can see you wearing glasses. The dinner pair. The meeting pair. The pair you reach for when it matters how you look. This is the layer most women have not consciously built. It is the layer Lorgner is most useful for.

Most women have the first two layers by accident. The third layer requires intention — and it is the one that changes how you feel about your collection.
