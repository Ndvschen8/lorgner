What Lorgner is

A personalized eyewear stylist
for your customers.

Lorgner is a personalized eyewear stylist that helps your customers decide which pair of glasses to wear for which occasion. Customers upload images of their current collection and the stylist engine recommends the perfect pair. When the engine identifies a gap it makes recommendations based on your product inventory.

Your customers just bought a few pairs of glasses with you — they walk away feeling great. Yet no one has helped them decide which pair to wear for which occasion.

Your customers come in primarily for their annual eye exam and decide to purchase one or two pairs of glasses. After the sale, that's it — you send them a reminder for their next eye exam. But what about the time in between? That's where Lorgner comes in. Customers upload pictures of their glasses and get advice on which pair to wear with which outfit and occasion. If there is a gap in their collection the engine recommends exactly the right frame from your product inventory, driving customers back to your shop for purchases in between exams.

How the eyewear stylist works:

  • Customers can upload up to 8 pairs of glasses
  • Customers engage with the chat engine asking which pair of glasses works with which outfit
  • The engine provides a personalized response based on their glasses — if there is a gap in their collection it recommends exactly the right frame from your product inventory
