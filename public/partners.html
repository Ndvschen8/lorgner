How white-label works
I
You provide your brand assets
Logo, color palette, brand voice, and your frame catalog. A simple spreadsheet with frame names and descriptions is enough. We do the rest.
II
We build your branded experience
Your name. Your colors. Your voice. Your frames. The AI learns your catalog and speaks in your register. No mention of Lorgner anywhere in the product.
III
It lives on your domain
A simple DNS record points yourbrand.com/stylist at our infrastructure. Your customers see only your domain. SSL handled automatically. Live within 5 business days.
IV
Your customers' data stays yours
All end-user data belongs to you. We never sell it, share it, or use it for any purpose beyond running the service. Full export available any time.
