How it works

Three steps.
One considered answer.

Lorgner is not a quiz, a filter, or a recommendation engine. It is an intelligence that looks at your actual glasses, understands their aesthetic, and reasons about the occasion you have described.

I
Introduce your collection
Upload photographs of the frames you own. Lorgner studies each pair — the silhouette, the material, the character. It remembers everything.
II
Describe the occasion
Tell Lorgner what you are dressing for. A board meeting, a charity dinner, a weekend morning, a video call that matters. As much or as little detail as you have.
III
Receive a considered answer
Lorgner recommends a specific pair with a real reason behind it. Not a score. Not a percentage. A reason — the kind a knowledgeable friend who has seen your collection would give you.
