From the Founder
"I'm amazed at how you always have glasses to match what you are wearing."

That is a compliment I get on a regular basis. And for a long time I didn't quite know what to do with it — because from the inside, it never felt like a system. It just felt like paying attention.

I had built up a collection over the years. The pair I wear to work. The ones I reach for on weekends. The frames I bought impulsively at a boutique and loved but never seemed to remember when I needed them. After a while, the collection grew faster than my ability to make use of it.

One morning I was getting ready and I realized I was reaching for the same pair again. Not because I liked it most — just because it was the one I always reached for. The rest of my glasses were sitting there, unworn, while I kept defaulting to one.

That felt wrong. I had invested in those frames. I had reasons for each of them. And I had no system for putting them to use.

So I built one. I started matching glasses to what I was wearing and where I was going — the way you'd think about shoes, or a bag, or a scarf. And the compliments kept coming. Not because I had different glasses than before, but because I was actually wearing them.

That is what Lorgner is. A system to help you wear the collection you already have. You upload your glasses, you tell us the occasion, and we tell you which pair fits. It is as simple as that.

I built this because I wanted other people to hear those same compliments — not because they bought different glasses, but because they started using the ones they already own.
